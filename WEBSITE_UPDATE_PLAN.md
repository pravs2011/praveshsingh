## Architectural & Technical Ground Rules

1. **Zero Framework Bloat / Pure Static Architecture:**
   - Maintain the existing performant, vanilla HTML5, custom CSS3 properties (dark theme design tokens: `--bg: #090b10`, `--bg-surface: #10141d`, `--primary: #6366f1`, `--teal: #0ea5e9`), and vanilla JavaScript.
   - No build tools, heavy bundlers, or client-side runtime frameworks.
2. **Anti-Hallucination & Verification Protocol:**
   - Never fabricate statistics, testimonials, client names, certifications, or project specifics.
   - Where real metrics or data are missing, use explicit placeholders: `[TODO: real number]`, `[TODO: testimonial]`, `[TODO: screenshot]`.
3. **Regulated Healthcare & Compliance Precision:**
   - Use exact, defensible phrasing: *"designed in line with ABDM guidelines and FHIR standards"* rather than unverified *"ABDM certified"*.
   - Never expose Patient Health Information (PHI) or identifiable clinical data in text, screenshots, or alt tags.
4. **Hub-and-Spoke Information Architecture:**
   - Transform `index.html` into a high-converting executive summary hub that routes visitors to dedicated, in-depth static pages (`/icare.html`, `/taskedge.html`, `/automation.html`, `/about.html`, `/expertise.html`, `/writing/index.html`).
5. **AI Search (AEO/GEO) & Technical SEO Leadership:**
   - Pair human-first readability with rich Schema.org JSON-LD graphs (`Person`, `Organization`, `SoftwareApplication`, `ProfessionalService`, `FAQPage`, `BreadcrumbList`, `Article`).
   - Maintain synchronized dual-tier machine context: `llms.txt` and `llms-full.txt`.

#### 2.1 `/icare.html` — Case Study: Digital Patient Registry & Medicine Inventory
- **Headline & Context:** Digital patient registry and cold-chain/clotting factor medicine inventory platform for rare blood disorders (Hemophilia & Thalassemia).
- **The Problem:** Fragmented clinical records across district hospitals, life-threatening stock-outs of high-cost factor concentrates, inability to verify treatment history during emergency inter-district patient travel.
- **Constraints:** Spotty 2G/3G network connectivity in district hospitals, non-technical clinic staff, legacy counter hardware, strict state health compliance and zero PHI tolerance on untrusted nodes.
- **What We Built:**
  - Real-time clotting factor concentrate inventory tracking with batch and expiry management.
  - Inter-hospital stock transfer workflows to prevent expiry and stock-outs.
  - Multi-tenant data segregation per treatment centre with aggregated state-level monitoring.
- **Architecture Diagram (Inline Clean SVG):**
  ```text
  [District Hospital Clients / PWA] ──(HTTPS/REST)──> [Nginx Reverse Proxy]
                                                              │
                                                       [Node.js / Express API]
                                                              │
                                               ┌──────────────┴──────────────┐
                                               ▼                             ▼
                                   [PostgreSQL DB]                [Audit Logging & Auth]
                             (Multi-Tenant Schema Isolation)     (RBAC, Token Auth, Logs)
  ```
- **Technical Decisions & Trade-offs:** Node.js/PostgreSQL chosen for relational transaction safety during stock transfers; lightweight client bundle for low-bandwidth operation; strict schema validation.
- **ABDM & Standards Roadmap:** Structured records designed in alignment with Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission (ABDM) milestones and FHIR data models.
- **Outcomes & Impact:** `[TODO: X centres live, Y patient visits recorded, Z stock transfers completed]`.

     - *Verification:* Confidence scoring, automated sanity checks, human-in-the-loop review queues.
