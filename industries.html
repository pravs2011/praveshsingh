Software built for the real world: Healthcare, SaaS & Public Sector.

Generic software often fails when it encounters industry-specific edge cases. Over 15+ years, I have architected systems tailored to stringent regulatory, operational, and multi-tenant domain constraints.

Domain 01

Healthcare & Life Sciences

Specialized architectures for patient longitudinal records, high-value cold-chain medicine inventory (Clotting Factor Concentrates), district hospital networks, and Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission (ABDM) integration pathways.

Explore iCare Case Study →
  • Batch & Expiry Ledger: Double-entry transactional tracking for high-value biological drugs across hospital dispensaries.
  • ABDM & FHIR Alignment: Designed to support ABHA ID verification and standardized FHIR JSON clinical bundles.
  • Offline-Tolerant Clinical UX: Resilient data collection for district hospital staff working with fluctuating network connectivity.
  • Zero PHI Exposure: Comprehensive audit logs, role-based access control (RBAC), and strict patient health privacy safeguards.
Domain 02

B2B SaaS & Growth Startups

Production-ready multi-tenant software architectures that scale cleanly without massive cloud overhead. Modular platforms designed for flexible business workflows, CRM, project tracking, and billing.

Explore TaskEdge SaaS →
  • Multi-Tenant Isolation: Robust PostgreSQL tenancy isolation models ensuring complete tenant data boundaries.
  • Modular Micro-Apps: Pluggable architecture supporting client CRM, task pipelines, time logging, and invoice generation.
  • Granular RBAC: Fine-grained permission trees matching complex corporate organizational hierarchies.
  • Webhook & Event Architecture: Asynchronous background job processing and reliable third-party API webhooks.
Domain 03

Enterprise Operations & Applied AI

Verifiable document intelligence, optical character recognition (OCR) pipelines, and compliance reporting systems for back-office enterprise operations.

Explore AI Solutions →
  • Document Intelligence: Multi-format extraction (PDF, scan, legacy spreadsheet) into strict, validated JSON Schemas.
  • Citation-Grounded RAG: Vector retrieval systems paired with direct paragraph citation bounding boxes.
