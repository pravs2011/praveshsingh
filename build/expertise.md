## Proof of Work

### iCare — SaaS · Multi-tenant · Healthcare

A digital patient registry and medicine inventory platform for hemophilia and thalassemia care,
deployed across treatment centres in Punjab and Madhya Pradesh.

- **Multi-tenant across states:** separate treatment centres share reporting while keeping patient
  data isolated.
- **Cross-hospital lookup:** a patient registered at one centre receives verified treatment at any
  connected centre.
- **Live inventory transfer:** surplus clotting factor stock moves between hospitals before a
  shortage becomes a clinical event.
- **Public-sector integration:** structured to ABDM guidelines with audit trails accepted by state
  health departments.

### TaskEdge — Operations SaaS

A multi-tenant web application that helps operations teams break work into items, assign
responsibility, and follow daily execution without heavyweight project management overhead.

- **Tenant isolation:** accounts, data, and permissions separated per organisation.
- **Fast status updates** with notification dispatch that stays responsive under load.
- **Shift handovers:** activity logs written for the person taking over, not for a project manager.
- **Low training cost:** designed so a supervisor can adopt it without a rollout programme.

### Practical data automation — Automation · Applied AI

Internal utilities that use language models and retrieval systems to remove repetitive administrative
data work, scoped to tasks where output can be checked.

- **Structured extraction** from scanned forms, invoices, and legacy paper records.
- **Retrieval pipelines (RAG)** grounded in verified technical manuals, not open-ended generation.
- **Format conversion** for government reporting requirements that would otherwise be retyped by hand.

## How an Engagement Runs

The same sequence whether the work is a full product build or a focused advisory review. Scope and
depth change; the order does not.

1. **Conversation** — a short call or exchange about the problem, the constraints, and what success
   looks like. No cost, and often enough to answer the question outright.
2. **Scope** — we agree what is being built or reviewed, what is explicitly out of scope, and the
   shape of the engagement, in writing before work starts.
3. **Architecture** — data model, interfaces, and deployment decided and documented first, so
   implementation is execution rather than discovery.
4. **Build & hand over** — delivered in working increments with review at each step, plus the
   documentation and runbook the team needs to run it without me.
