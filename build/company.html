State Health · Punjab & Madhya Pradesh

State-deployed patient registry and biological medicine inventory platform for hemophilia and thalassemia care: portable cross-hospital patient profiles, double-entry Factor VIII/IX ledgers, FEFO allocation, ABDM-aligned FHIR R4 exports, and live analytics for state health commissioners. Demonstrated live at the National Hemophilia Consensus Meeting (Bhopal, 2025).

AI-powered business operating system for deskless and shift-based teams: true tenant isolation, sub-50ms task updates, structured shift handovers, integrated proposals/invoicing/expenses, and granular audit trails.
