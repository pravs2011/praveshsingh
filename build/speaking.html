Talks, workshops & state health reviews

Pravesh Singh has been invited to present iCare and digital health architecture before medical college faculty, state health mission directors, and hospital staff across Punjab, Madhya Pradesh, and Chandigarh — including live, on-stage demonstrations of production systems handling real patient data.

Summary

Pravesh Singh has delivered four invited health-tech presentations between 2023 and 2025: a live multi-hospital iCare demonstration at the National Hemophilia Consensus Meeting (Bhopal, May 2025), an NHM Punjab capacity-building workshop with Mission Director Sh. Ghanshyam Thori, IAS (Chandigarh, November 2024), a guest-of-honour keynote at the Hemophilia & Thalassemia Summit (Faridkot, November 2023), and hands-on digital-intake training for PSACS & NACO staff (Chandigarh, July 2025).
