Successful Workshop with PSACS – Empowering Healthcare with Technology

I had the incredible opportunity to conduct a workshop for Punjab State AIDS Control Society (PSACS), focused on how iCare can revolutionize patient registration and drug management in healthcare settings.

📊 Through a detailed presentation and live demonstration, we showcased how iCare enables:

Seamless digital patient registration
Efficient drug inventory & distribution tracking
Better data transparency and reporting
Support for ABDM compliance & integrations

🧑‍⚕️ The session was attended by committed healthcare professionals working across various disease programs, including HIV/AIDS, Hemophilia, and Thalassemia. Their engagement and feedback were truly encouraging.

📸 Sharing a few snapshots from the event — it’s an honor to contribute to the digital transformation of healthcare services in Punjab.

🙏 Huge thanks to the PSACS team for the opportunity and warm support. Looking forward to continued collaboration in making patient care more efficient, accessible, and data-driven.
