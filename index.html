Founder & Engineering Philosophy

Writing software that fits clinic and workplace reality.

Code has to survive the messy reality of day-to-day operations: spotty hospital networks, busy clinic staff, and urgent patient transfers.

I lead software engineering as Founder & CTO at SoftEdge Technology Solutions, where we build web applications, APIs, cloud platforms, and AI automation systems for organizations in healthcare, SaaS, and enterprise operations.

A large part of my career has involved building healthcare software alongside clinical teams and state health administrators. Through our work on iCare with the National Health Mission (NHM) Punjab, the Punjab State AIDS Control Society (PSACS), and health authorities in Madhya Pradesh, I have spent significant time inside hospitals understanding how doctors, nurses, and pharmacists actually track treatments and factor concentrate inventories.

When emergency medicine is involved, theoretical software architectures do not help if a nurse cannot quickly verify a patient's dose history or if a pharmacist cannot locate available medicine nearby. That experience shapes how I build all systems: keeping interfaces clear, minimizing dependencies, making database queries predictable, and verifying that every screen serves an immediate operational need.

Healthcare Information Technology

iCare Healthcare Platform

A mission-critical digital registry and medicine inventory platform designed for rare blood disorders (Hemophilia & Thalassemia). Patients require periodic infusions of clotting factor concentrates; if a hospital runs out of stock, delays can lead to irreversible joint damage or life-threatening hemorrhage.

  • Portable patient profiles: A patient registered at one district hospital can receive verified care and factor concentrate at any connected centre statewide without re-entering history.
  • Real-time inventory & inter-centre stock transfers: Tracks vial batch numbers, expiry dates, and real-time cold-chain stock levels, allowing centres with surplus factor to transfer stock before expiry.
  • ABDM & FHIR aligned architecture: Built with role-based access control, cryptographic integrity, encrypted storage, and immutable audit logs aligned with Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission guidelines.
  • Statewide health intelligence: Provides state program officers and health commissioners with live visibility into patient epidemiology, consumption rates, and supply-chain health.
